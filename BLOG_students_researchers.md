## 2. Research Mode — A Full Academic Paper in One Click

Regular AI chat gives you an answer. Research Mode gives you a paper.

Dynamo AI's Research Mode runs a **3-model pipeline** behind the scenes:

1. **Live web search** pulls the most current sources on your topic
2. **Claude** extracts key findings, statistics, and evidence
3. **Gemini** identifies themes, contradictions, and underexplored angles
4. **GPT** synthesises everything into a structured academic paper

The output: a properly structured paper with an abstract, literature review, methodology discussion, analysis, and conclusion — all in one go.

**Choose your citation format:** APA 7th, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, or Vancouver — and the paper comes out with correctly formatted in-text citations and a full reference list. No manual formatting.

This is what took days. Now it takes minutes.
