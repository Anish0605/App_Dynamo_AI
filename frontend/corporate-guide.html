Intelligence Tiers

AI Modes

Dynamo AI has four distinct intelligence modes. Choose the right one based on how much depth you need and how quickly you need it.

⚡ Fast Mode
Default <5 sec Free

Instant Answers

Powered by Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite — the fastest available AI model. Perfect for quick definitions, calculations, summaries, or drafting short emails and messages. No waiting.

VSE Example

"What is the difference between EPC and HAM project models?" — answered in 3 seconds with a clear comparison.

🧠 DeepThink
PRO 15–30 sec Built-in reasoning

Deep Analytical Reasoning

Uses Gemini 3 Flash with built-in thinking — the model reasons step-by-step before giving its answer. ~5× more intelligent than Fast Mode. Ideal for complex engineering calculations, legal clause analysis, financial modelling, and project risk assessment.

VSE Example

"Analyse the risk factors in a HAM project with a 15-year concession period in a Tier-3 city. What financial mitigations should we propose?" — structured risk report with mitigations in under 30 seconds.

🔬 Research Mode
PLUS 60–90 sec Web + 3 AI models

Multi-Model Research Pipeline

A sequential pipeline of three AI models — Claude Sonnet for extraction, Gemini for analysis, GPT for final writing — combined with live Tavily web search. Produces sourced, structured research papers with proper academic citations (IEEE, APA, Harvard, Vancouver, and more).

VSE Example

"Write a research report on emerging cable-stayed bridge construction techniques adopted in Indian highway projects 2022–2025 with IEEE citations." — Full paper with 12+ citations in 90 seconds.

🤖 Deep Research Agent
PRO 3–8 min Gemini Interactions API

Autonomous Research Agent

The most powerful mode. Powered by Google's own Deep Research engine (the same technology behind Google's Deep Research product). The agent autonomously plans a research strategy, runs ~80 targeted web searches, reads academic papers, extracts data, identifies gaps, and synthesises a comprehensive referenced report — all without any manual input. Reports are saved automatically to your Document Library.

VSE Example

"Conduct a full investigation into urban ropeway technology adoption globally — current projects, technical standards, maintenance costs, passenger capacity benchmarks, and future directions." — 8-page referenced report delivered in under 8 minutes.
