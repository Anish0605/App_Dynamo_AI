How to Use Dynamo AI

Master every feature with our step-by-step guide. Learn how to unlock the full power of AI research.

🎧 Radio Mode - Listen to Responses

Transform your research into an immersive audio experience. Perfect for commutes, workouts, or when you prefer to listen instead of read.

How to Use:

  1. 1. Click the Tools button in the input bar
  2. 2. Toggle "🎧 Radio Mode" on
  3. 3. Type your question and send
  4. 4. AI response plays automatically as audio
  5. 5. Control playback with play/pause buttons

Benefits:

  • Hands-free learning while on-the-go
  • Natural speech synthesis for better understanding
  • Adjustable playback speed
  • Works in dark mode for late-night study

🔍 Web Search - Real-Time Information

Get the latest information from the internet. Perfect for current events, latest trends, breaking news, and up-to-date data.

How to Use:

  1. 1. Click Tools button
  2. 2. Under "Research Tools", enable Web Search
  3. 3. Ask any question about recent events
  4. 4. Dynamo searches the web and provides fresh answers
  5. 5. Sources are cited in the response

Example Queries:

  • "Latest AI news March 2024"
  • "Bitcoin price today"
  • "Current weather trends"
  • "Breaking news today"

🎤 Voice Input - Speak Your Query

Use voice to ask questions instead of typing. Great for quick queries and hands-free interaction.

How to Use:

  1. 1. Click the 🎤 mic button (red icon)
  2. 2. Speak your question clearly
  3. 3. Voice is transcribed to text
  4. 4. Click send or wait for auto-send
  5. 5. Get instant response

Tips:

  • Speak slowly and clearly
  • Ensure microphone permissions are granted
  • Works best in quiet environments
  • Edit transcription before sending if needed

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Dynamo AI offline?

Dynamo AI requires an internet connection to function as it connects to AI servers. However, your history is saved locally.

How much data can I upload at once?

You can upload documents up to 25MB per file. For best results, keep files under 10MB for faster processing.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes! Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We don't share your information with third parties. Conversations are tied to your account only.

What's the difference between Fast and Research modes?

Fast Mode: Instant responses, great for brainstorming. Research Mode: More detailed analysis, perfect with Web Search and DeepThink for comprehensive research.

How do I contact support?

Click on your profile (bottom of sidebar) and select "Support". You can also email us directly or reach out through social media @dynamoai_in
