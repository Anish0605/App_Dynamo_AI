Step 7

How to Chat

The main chat area handles everything — questions, file analysis, formatted answers, code, citations, and more.

Typing a message

  1. 1Click the text box and type your question.
  2. 2Press Enter or click the ↑ send button.
  3. 3Dynamo responds with formatted markdown — headings, bullet points, code, tables, and citations all rendered beautifully.

Uploading a file

  1. 1Click the + button → Add photos & files.
  2. 2Choose a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or image from your device.
  3. 3Type your question about it, e.g. "Summarise this paper" or "Find the key arguments".
  4. 4A "Remember this" chip appears — click it to save the file to your Document Library for future sessions.

Web Search

  1. 1Click + → toggle Web search on.
  2. 2Ask anything about current events, latest research, prices, news.
  3. 3Dynamo searches Tavily, pulls live results, and answers with cited sources.
Quantum computing overview
Explain quantum entanglement for my exam tomorrow

Quantum Entanglement — Exam Guide

Definition: Two particles are entangled when the quantum state of one cannot be described independently of the other, regardless of distance.

Key Exam Points:

  • Measuring one instantly affects the other
  • No faster-than-light communication (Einstein's concern)
  • Used in quantum cryptography & computing
Step 8

Research Features

Dynamo AI has a dedicated academic research pipeline — Research Mode, Deep Research Agent, Citation Checker, and Research Watcher.

🔬

Research Mode PLUS

3-model pipeline — Claude → Gemini → GPT-5.4 with live web search

Stage 1 — Extract

Claude Sonnet reads 10+ live web sources and extracts the key facts and arguments.

Stage 2 — Analyse

Gemini synthesises the findings, spots patterns, and identifies academic context.

Stage 3 — Write

GPT-5.4 writes the final report in your chosen citation format (APA, MLA, IEEE, etc.)

💡 How to use: Click + → select Research Mode → type your research topic → click send.
🤖

Deep Research Agent PRO

Autonomous agent — browses the web, evaluates sources, writes a literature review (3–8 min)

  1. 1Click + → Deep Research Agent
  2. 2Type your research topic
  3. 3Watch live progress updates appear in the chat as the agent browses and analyses
  4. 4A full, cited research report appears when complete — download as DOCX
📎

Citation Checker PLUS

Verify, fix and reformat citations in 8 academic formats

Supported formats:
APA 7th · MLA 9th · Chicago 17th · Harvard · IEEE · Vancouver · ACS · ASA
What it does:
Checks DOI validity, fixes formatting errors, converts between styles, and flags broken references.
💡 Find it in the sidebar under Quick Tools → Export / Save or type "check my citations" in chat.
👁️

Research Watcher PRO

Get daily email digests when new research appears on your topics

  1. 1In the sidebar, click Quick Tools → Smart Actions → Watch a topic
  2. 2Type your research topic (e.g. "AI safety 2026")
  3. 3Every 24 hours, Dynamo searches the web and emails you if genuinely new content appears

Step 13

Organising Chats & Folders

Keep your research organised by grouping chats into named folders — perfect for different subjects or projects.

Creating a folder

  1. 1Click the Folders tab in the sidebar.
  2. 2Click the + New Folder button.
  3. 3Type a name (e.g. "Thesis 2026", "Physics Notes").
  4. 4Press Enter to save.

Moving a chat to a folder

  1. 1In the Chats tab, hover over a chat.
  2. 2Click the ⋯ menu that appears.
  3. 3Select Move to folder and choose your folder.
💡 Tip: The Chats tab always shows ALL your chats (including those in folders) as a flat list — great for finding recent conversations quickly.
