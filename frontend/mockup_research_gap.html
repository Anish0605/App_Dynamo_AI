Explain the current state of quantum computing and its applications

🔬 Research Analysis

Quantum computing leverages quantum mechanical phenomena—superposition and entanglement—to process information in fundamentally different ways than classical computers. Current systems from IBM, Google, and IonQ have demonstrated quantum advantage in specific tasks.

  • IBM's 433-qubit Osprey processor (2022) marked a major milestone
  • Google's Sycamore performed a calculation in 200 seconds (classical: 10,000 years)
  • Key applications: drug discovery, cryptography, optimization, financial modelling
  • Major challenge remains: quantum error correction and decoherence

Research Gap Analysis
Unexplored angles identified by Gemini
Gap 1 — Quantum Computing in Developing Economies
Most research focuses on US/EU/China implementations. The potential for quantum computing adoption in India, Brazil, and Southeast Asia—including infrastructure challenges—is almost entirely unstudied.
Gap 2 — Hybrid Classical-Quantum Pipelines
The transition period (2025–2030) where businesses combine classical and quantum hardware is under-researched. How do organizations manage this hybrid architecture in practice?
Gap 3 — Long-term Workforce Impact
Literature on job displacement vs. job creation caused by quantum computing is sparse. No longitudinal studies exist on skills retraining for quantum roles.
